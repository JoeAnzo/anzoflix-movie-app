# Brag Plan: AnzoFlix

## What is this app?
AnzoFlix is a movie and TV discovery app for browsing titles, saving favorites, building a watchlist, and opening a title's playback screen.

## The angle
Make the familiar ritual of finding something to watch feel like a tiny cinema premiere. Start with the project's own red-curtain image, then move through the real app journey: browse movie rows, choose a title, save it or add it to a watchlist, and open its watch screen. Keep the edit premium and direct; the product UI is the hero.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Red theater curtains and the AnzoFlix wordmark establish the cinema mood immediately. The line “Find your next watch.” lands over the curtain before the app UI takes over.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The home screen's real “Movies,” “Now Playing,” “Trending,” and “Upcoming” browse sections appear as a compact, poster-led feed.
- A title detail screen brings forward the actual “Save,” “Add to Watchlist,” and “Watch” actions.
- The watch route appears with the app's real “Checking authorized playback sources…” state, making the final step in the journey clear without implying that a sample title is currently streaming.

## Outro / punchline
The watch screen resolves into a clean AnzoFlix end card: “Browse. Save. Watch.” No availability or catalog-size claims.

## User flow worth showing
Open the home feed → choose a title and save it or add it to the watchlist → tap Watch to enter its playback screen. A fictional title, “Afterglow,” is used only as a privacy-safe visual stand-in; no real catalog metadata or playback is claimed.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: a quiet, premium movie-night premiere
- Interpretation: clean feature-forward framing, comfortable holds, crisp UI transitions, and restrained sound; the app's real labels carry the story.
